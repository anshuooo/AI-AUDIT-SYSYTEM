# Metrics

## Primary success metrics

- **Audit submission rate** — percentage of visitors who complete an audit.
- **Lead capture rate** — proportion of audit viewers who submit contact details.
- **Shareable report click-through** — views from public report links.
- **Email confirmation delivery** — successful transactional email sends.

## Product health metrics

- **Total monthly savings calculated** — aggregate estimated cost savings delivered to users.
- **Report engagement time** — average time spent on the public audit page.
- **Repeat audit frequency** — number of returning users generating multiple audits.
- **Error and exception rate** — server-side failures per audit request.

## Growth and marketing metrics

- **Public report share ratio** — share views per audit creation.
- **Conversion from audit to lead** — qualified lead volume from completed audit flows.
- **CI pass rate** — percentage of successful backend test and lint runs.
- **Deployment velocity** — feature release cadence while maintaining stability.

## Founder metrics

- **Projected savings closed** — dollar value of savings opportunities converted to paid work.
- **Customer acquisition efficiency** — cost to acquire a lead from audit users.
- **Product-market fit signal** — ratio of audit viewers requesting follow-up.
- **Retention signal** — users who return for another audit within 30 days.
