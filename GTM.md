# Go-To-Market Strategy

## Target audience

- Early-stage founders and product leaders managing AI software stacks
- Finance and operations teams responsible for SaaS spend optimization
- AI-native companies with multiple licenses across ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, and productivity tooling

## Value proposition

AI Audit System delivers a fast, credible snapshot of AI spend efficiency. Customers can discover plan mismatches, unlock savings, and share a polished report with decision-makers.

## Messaging pillars

1. **Save more from your AI stack** — find wasted spend and optimize tool tiers.
2. **Make AI budgets measurable** — translate license usage into dollar savings.
3. **Share insights instantly** — public report links and exportable summaries.

## Launch channels

- Product Hunt launch with founder story and savings case study
- LinkedIn posts targeted at AI leaders and SaaS operators
- Hacker News discussion comparing AI spend tools
- Email outreach to startup operators and finance teams

## Growth tactics

- Offer a free audit report in exchange for email capture
- Use shareable report links to drive self-serve referrals
- Partner with finance and procurement communities for trust and distribution

## Early traction metrics

- Lead conversion from audit submission
- Public report view rate
- Follow-up request volume
- Clickthrough from audit insights to email capture
