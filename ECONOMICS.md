# Economics

## Business model

AI Audit System is positioned as a freemium audit tool with optional advisory or subscription upsell. The core product is a low-friction audit report, and revenue is generated through paid optimization consultations, enterprise licensing, and partner integrations.

## Revenue streams

- **Lead-to-consulting conversion** — capture audit leads and convert them into paid optimization engagements.
- **Premium subscriptions** — monthly access to continuous audit monitoring and AI spend dashboards.
- **Partner referrals** — integration partnerships with SaaS procurement platforms and finance advisory firms.

## Unit economics

- **Customer acquisition cost** should remain low by using organic channels and viral sharing.
- **Average deal size** for advisory engagements can start at $2,500 for a single AI stack optimization review.
- **Marginal cost** is minimal for audit generation and report delivery, making the platform highly scalable.

## Cost structure

- Hosting for frontend and backend
- MongoDB storage and operational costs
- Email delivery expenses for lead confirmations
- CI and maintenance overhead

## Value capture

Each audit report should justify the product value by showing a minimum of 3x run-rate savings compared to subscription costs. The product can charge a small engagement fee or premium membership while still delivering a strong ROI for customers.

## Go-to-market economics

- Publish free audits to generate warm leads.
- Use shareable public reports to expand network effects.
- Capture enterprise prospects with higher-touch follow-up workflows.
